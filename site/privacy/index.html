Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What the extension does

RekodJa: Job Tracker is a Chrome extension that saves job-posting details into a Google Sheet you choose. When you open the extension on a job page, it suggests the company and role from the page title and job-posting metadata. You review the details before saving them with the job link and application date.

Information the extension accesses

  • Google Sheets: with your permission through Google OAuth, the extension reads and writes the spreadsheet you configure. It reads sheet-tab information so you can select a tab, and can add job records, set up headers, and apply formatting in that spreadsheet.
  • Current tab: when you open the extension, it reads the active page URL, title, and relevant metadata to suggest job details. It does not monitor browsing in the background.
  • Settings: the spreadsheet ID and selected sheet-tab name are saved in Chrome extension storage.

Google account permissions

The extension requests Google’s spreadsheets permission. This permission can technically allow access to your Google spreadsheets; the extension’s implementation uses the spreadsheet ID you provide. It does not browse or list your Drive files. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts, or Calendar, and has no background worker.

How information is used and shared

Job details and spreadsheet requests go directly from your browser to Google’s servers to provide the features you use. We do not operate a backend server for the extension or collect your job records on our own servers. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with other third parties.

Storage and retention

Your job records remain in the Google Sheet you control until you edit or delete them. Your spreadsheet ID and selected tab name are stored using Chrome’s synced extension storage, which may sync across your signed-in Chrome browsers according to your Chrome settings. These settings are not sent to us. Google handles the connected Google services under its own policies.

Revoking access and deleting records

You can revoke the extension’s Google access at any time from Google Account permissions, and uninstall the extension to stop using it. Revoking access or uninstalling does not delete previously saved rows: delete those records directly in your Google Sheet.

The companion web app

This policy covers the RekodJa Chrome extension. The companion RekodJa Web App is a separate product with its own privacy policy at app.rekodja.com/privacy.
