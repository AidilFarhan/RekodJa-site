Who we are and what the extension does

RekodJa: Job Tracker (“RekodJa”, “the extension”, “we”) is a Chrome extension developed and operated by Aidil Farhan, an individual developer based in Malaysia. It saves job-posting details into a Google Sheet that you choose. When you open the extension on a job page, it suggests the company and role from the page title and job-posting metadata. You review the details before saving them together with the job link and application date.

Information the extension accesses

  • Google user data (Google Sheets): with your permission through Google OAuth, the extension reads and writes the spreadsheet you configure. See “Google user data we access” below for the exact data types and purposes.
  • Current tab: when you open the extension, it reads the active page’s URL, title and relevant job-posting metadata to suggest job details. This is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to us. The extension does not monitor your browsing in the background.
  • Settings: the spreadsheet ID and the selected sheet-tab name are saved in Chrome extension storage.

Google user data we access

The extension requests exactly one Google OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets (See, edit, create and delete your Google Sheets spreadsheets)

We use this scope only to provide the features you ask for, and only on the spreadsheet whose ID you provide:

  • Sheet and tab information: the spreadsheet’s tab names, so you can choose which tab to save into.
  • Header row: to check whether the header row exists, and to create or repair it if needed. The extension does not overwrite a custom header row you already have.
  • Job records: to append the rows you choose to save (company, role, job URL, date applied, status, source) and to update the status of a record you change.
  • Formatting: to apply date formats, status and source dropdowns, colour rules and the days-since-applied formula in that spreadsheet.

Google describes this scope broadly, so it can technically allow access to any of your spreadsheets. RekodJa’s implementation only uses the spreadsheet ID you provide. It does not list or browse your Google Drive files, and it does not read other spreadsheets. It does not request access to Gmail, Contacts or Calendar, and it has no background worker.

How Google user data is used and shared

Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above. It is sent directly between your browser and Google’s servers. We do not operate a backend server for the extension, and we do not receive, copy or store your Google user data or your job records on any server of ours.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not use it for advertising, including personalised or retargeted ads. We do not transfer or share it with third parties, except where necessary to comply with applicable law. No human reads your data, and it is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

RekodJa’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between the extension and Google’s APIs uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • No token handling by us: Google OAuth access tokens are obtained and managed by Chrome’s identity system. They are not sent to us, logged by us or stored by us.
  • No central copy: because there is no RekodJa server or database, your job records exist only in your own Google Sheet, protected by your Google account’s security (including any 2-step verification you enable).
  • Least privilege: the extension asks for a single Google scope and a minimal set of browser permissions (identity, activeTab, scripting, storage, and access to sheets.googleapis.com), and it only reads the current page when you open it.
  • No credentials or keys in the extension: it does not ask for or store your Google password.

No system is perfectly secure, but the design above means there is no RekodJa-operated store of your data that could be breached.

Storage and retention

Your job records remain in the Google Sheet you control until you edit or delete them. Your spreadsheet ID and selected tab name are stored using Chrome’s synced extension storage, which may sync across your signed-in Chrome browsers according to your Chrome settings. These settings are not sent to us. RekodJa retains no Google user data of its own. Google handles the connected Google services under its own policies.

Revoking access and deleting your data

You can revoke the extension’s Google access at any time from Google Account permissions, and uninstall the extension to stop using it. Uninstalling the extension also removes its locally stored settings from Chrome. Revoking access or uninstalling does not delete rows already saved: delete those records directly in your Google Sheet. Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing further for us to delete; if you have any doubt, contact us and we will confirm.

Children

RekodJa is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
