Simple by design

From job listing to tracker
in five small steps.

Set up your own Google Sheet once, then save applications while you browse.

01

Add to Chrome

Install RekodJa from the Chrome Web Store and pin it in your toolbar.

Get extension →
02

Connect your Google Sheet

Paste your Sheet ID in settings, select the tab for applications, and save.

docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/YOUR_SHEET_ID/edit
03

Open an individual job posting

On LinkedIn, JobStreet, or Indeed, open a page for one specific role — not a search-results page.

04

Click the RekodJa extension

Review the job link and add your application date before saving.

05

Save, then stay on top of every application

One click adds the job to your sheet. Open the web app for follow-up reminders, email drafts, and a complete overview.

Your data, your control

Your job search never
leaves your hands.

Privacy is not an add-on. It is the way RekodJa is built.

No developer server

Minimum permissions

RekodJa requests only the Google permissions needed to write to the sheet you select.
